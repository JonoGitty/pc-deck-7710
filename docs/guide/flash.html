Chapter 8

Flash it, and read the four-stage self-test. Everything here happens on a desk, from USB, and risks nothing but your time.

# the whole thing, guided
python3 tools/deckctl.py

# or step by step
python3 tools/deckctl.py doctor
python3 tools/deckctl.py build   --display ssd1322
python3 tools/deckctl.py flash
python3 tools/deckctl.py logs

doctor first, always: it says what is missing from your toolchain and how to get it, and it reads the chip ID over USB — which is where an ESP32-S3 bought by mistake gets caught, before any of it is your fault.

First light: the self-test is staged for a reason

A blank panel has six causes and one symptom, so the boot self-test walks up the stack and each stage tells you something the previous one could not.

Stage 1 — the driver

A pattern written straight from the panel driver, before core/ is involved at all. Blank here is hardware: wiring, power, or the wrong controller.

Stage 2 — the output stage

The intensity levels through the quantiser. If stage 1 drew and this does not, the panel is fine and the level mapping is not.

Stage 3 — the font

Text from the ROM. Proves the generated font header made it into the image and reads correctly.

Stage 4 — the subsystems

The table of what came up: Bluetooth, tuner, mux, buttons. Health is four states — unknown means not tried yet, which is not failed.

Blank at stage 1 is hardware. Blank after stage 1 is software. That single distinction is the whole reason it is staged, and it is the difference between an evening with a multimeter and an evening with a debugger.

Then pair it. Your phone's own Bluetooth menu — no app, no pairing code, nothing to install. Play something and the analyser moves. If the deck does not appear, deckctl logs says whether Bluetooth came up at all, which is a different problem from it coming up and not being found.

⚠️ The firmware compiles and has never run on hardware. The self-test is written and its stages are simulated on the host by tools/sim/, which is not the same as having lit a panel with it.
