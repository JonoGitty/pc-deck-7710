Choose a donor. You are buying a box, a face, a cage and a bag of buttons — so buy a broken one.

The chassis comes from a scrap head unit — buy a broken one

You are buying a box, a face, a cage and a bag of buttons. The CD mechanism, the amplifier, the tuner and the microcontroller all go in the bin on the first evening — so a jammed unit at £8 is worth as much to you as a working one at £40. A working CD mechanism is the biggest driver of price on a listing and the first thing you remove.
